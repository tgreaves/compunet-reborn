A directory can show your own PETSCII above its list of entries, in place of the standard frame.

WHAT THE FILE MUST BE

A raw PETSCII sequence — the frame body only, with no load address and no frame header. This is what the editor writes and what a .seq from a PETSCII art tool contains.

  • At most {{ max_bytes }} bytes.
  • It may only draw on rows 0 to 5. Row 6 is the top of the directory frame, and anything lower overwrites the entry list.
  • No clear-screen. The header is drawn on top of the directory, so clearing the screen erases the whole thing.
  • No lower-case switch, and turn reverse off before the end — otherwise the rest of the screen inherits it.

If a file breaks any of these, it is refused and the reason says which byte is at fault. Nothing you upload is ever altered to fit.
