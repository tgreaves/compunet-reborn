3D-PRINTED LOGO

A desk plaque of the Compunet logo, for multi-colour printers.

Download

compunet-logo-prepared.3mf Print-ready Bambu Studio project. Use this one. Plated, coloured and set up — open it and print
compunet-logo.3mf The plain generated model, with no printer settings — for a different slicer, or to change the design
compunet-logo.stl Single colour, everything merged — for previewing, or a one-filament print

Printing it

Both files carry two variants. Raised stands the lettering 2.5 mm proud of the plate (pictured); flush inlays the colour into the top face so the surface is one continuous plane. Delete whichever you don't want before slicing.

The prepared project already has everything below set. It's here so you know what you're getting, and for anyone slicing the plain model themselves.

Size212 × 42 mm, 15 mm thick — heavy enough to stand on its long edge
FilamentsThree: light grey, red, blue (the authentic C64 palette values)
SupportsNone. It's a flat slab — print face-up
Infill15% — nothing structural happens below the top layer
Colour changesOne, near the top. All three colours share the final layers, so the AMS purges four times, not throughout

Slicing compunet-logo.3mf instead? Bambu Studio will warn that it isn't a Bambu Lab project and load "geometry and colour data only" — that's expected, and the colours still come through. The prepared project opens without the warning. The generator and full printing notes are in the repository.
